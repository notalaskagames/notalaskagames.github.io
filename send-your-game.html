Send us your game

Made a co-op horror game or something weird? Send it over. I play these badly with friends, using my own mods that make them fall apart. Lately that meant a cat army and way too much dynamite.

How to send it

On Steam, send it through Curator Connect to the NotAlaska Games curator page. No keys needed.

On Discord, drop your Steam link and a one-line pitch in #send-us-your-game. Don't post keys there, I'll DM you.

What helps

How many players it takes and any embargo date.

What happens next

If we make a video, you get the link when it goes up. No promise of a video or a date, and fair warning, I'll probably mod it.
